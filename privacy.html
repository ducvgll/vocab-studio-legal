Privacy Policy

Vocab Studio · Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. About this app

Vocab Studio is a personal tool used by its owner to create and publish the owner's own English-vocabulary videos to social media accounts that the owner controls, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name) through TikTok Login Kit, and uses the Content Posting API only to upload or publish videos that the owner created.

3. How information is used

Access tokens are used solely to publish the owner's own videos on the owner's own account. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

Tokens and settings are stored locally on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, other than sending the owner's videos and captions to the platforms the owner chooses to publish on.

5. Other users' data

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other user or visitor.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete stored tokens at any time by removing them from the app settings, and can revoke the app's access in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services.
